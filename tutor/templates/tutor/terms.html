📋 What this session collects

MetaChat Tutor is a research instrument: it teaches constructive communication roles in online group discussion, and it records how learners do. This page is the plain record of what is stored.

Collected at intake

Your name, your group, and the level you pick for yourself. These open the session and label the research record; there is no account, no e-mail, and no password.

Collected while you work

Every message you send, the tutor replies, the state you answered in, your pretest and posttest answers with their scores, and the completion status of each task. Every entry carries the state it was produced in. The data collection step at the end adds the name you give there.

What you can take with you

The export button downloads this session as one JSON file named after its session id. Reset erases the browser copy of the session; the research record keeps its row, because the row is the measurement.

Who reads it

The research team behind MetaChat Tutor, for analysis of linguodidactics outcomes. Names match a learner to their own cohort, and are not used to publish them.
